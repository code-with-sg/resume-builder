Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and aim to keep your resume creation experience simple and transparent.

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SKY GRIT. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit our website and use our free online Resume Builder.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information You Enter

Our Resume Builder allows you to enter information such as:

  • Full name and contact details.
  • City and state.
  • Educational qualifications.
  • Work experience and employment details.
  • Skills, languages and other resume information.

This information is used by the Resume Builder to create your resume preview and generate a downloadable PDF.

3. Browser-Based Processing

Our Resume Builder is designed to process the information you enter directly in your web browser.

We do not intentionally upload your resume information to a SKY GRIT application server as part of the normal resume creation process.

Your resume information is not intentionally stored in a SKY GRIT database through the current builder. It generally remains in the active browser session unless you choose to save or share the generated file.

However, this does not mean that every part of your website visit is private from third parties. Analytics, advertising and external services may collect technical information as described below.

4. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website infrastructure or third-party services.

This may include:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and interaction information.
  • Referral sources and visit duration.

This information may be used for website analytics, security, performance monitoring and advertising.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website and to improve the user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as browser details, device information, page views and website interaction data. It may use cookies or similar technologies depending on its configuration.

Google's collection and processing of information are governed by its own privacy policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party advertising services, including Monetag, to display advertisements and support the operation of our free website.

These advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to display advertisements, measure advertising performance and prevent invalid activity.

Such providers may collect technical information, including IP address, browser information and device data, subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

We do not control the independent data collection practices of third-party advertising providers.

7. PDF Generation

Our website uses the html2pdf.js JavaScript library to generate downloadable PDF resumes in your browser.

The library is loaded from a third-party content delivery network (CDN). Your browser may connect to that provider to download the library.

The PDF generation process is performed in your browser using the information entered into the Resume Builder.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, advertising, security and website functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some third-party services or website functionality.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell the resume information you enter in the builder to third parties.

However, technical information may be processed by third-party analytics, advertising, hosting and infrastructure providers as necessary for their services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

10. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to maintain the security and proper functioning of our website.

Browser-based processing helps reduce the need to transmit resume details to our application server. However, no website, browser or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid using shared or public devices for sensitive personal information and close your browser session when finished.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings and use available controls offered by third-party advertising providers.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is a general-purpose resume creation tool and is not specifically directed at children. Users should use the service in accordance with applicable age and privacy laws.

13. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content or practices of external websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of those websites before sharing information.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised Last Updated date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our information-handling practices, please contact the SKY GRIT website operator through the official contact channel provided on our website.
