HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our free online resume builder.

1. Is the SKY GRIT | SG Resume Builder free?

Yes. You can create your resume, choose a template and download it as a PDF for free.

2. How can I create a resume online?

Enter your personal information, education and skills. You can also add work experience and other optional details, select a resume template and generate your resume preview.

3. Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. After generating your resume preview, click the Download Resume PDF button to save your resume in PDF format.

4. Which resume templates are available?

You can choose from three designs: Classic, Modern and ATS Friendly.

5. Can freshers create a resume without work experience?

Yes. Work experience is optional. Freshers and students can create a resume using their education, skills and other relevant details.

6. Do I need to create an account?

No account creation is required to use the resume builder.

7. Can I add my own career objective?

Yes. You can enter your own career objective or professional summary. If you leave it blank, the builder uses a default summary.

8. Is the resume builder suitable for Indian job seekers?

Yes. The form includes Indian States and Union Territories, along with common education qualifications and language options.
